How it works

Three steps to beautiful previews

01
Make an API call

Pass your title, description, and optional template as query params.

GET /og?title=My+Post&key=sk_xxx
02
Renders at the edge

SnapOG processes on Cloudflare Workers — sub-100ms, globally distributed.

← PNG 1200×630 (cached in R2)
03
Serve as og:image

Drop the URL into your HTML head. Twitter, LinkedIn, Slack — everywhere reads it perfectly.

<meta property="og:image" content="..." />
Integration

Add to your <head>
in 30 seconds

Two meta tags. No SDK, no build step, no dependencies. Paste the URL, ship your post, watch the preview render flawlessly on every platform. 

<!-- Add to your <head> -->

<meta property="og:image"
  content="https://snapog.dev/og?title=My+Post&key=sk_xxx" />

<meta name="twitter:image"
  content="https://snapog.dev/og?title=My+Post&key=sk_xxx" />
